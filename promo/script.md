# Chromasmith — Square One / v8 (series)

## Direction

A series of short films (10–20s each) instead of one long one. Each episode tells one idea. Every episode **starts and ends on the same frame**: the real Chromasmith logo on paper, with the red square sitting in its slot. The square pops out, does its job, and drops back home.

The hero is the red square from the logo, animated like a Pixar character: anticipation before every move, squash on landing, stretch on take-off, follow-through wobble, a corner lean when it "looks" at something. It has no face; its posture tells the story.

Earlier versions are archived in `archive/` (v6 single film, v7 60s film).

## Production approach — pilot first, then expand

1. **Pilot: Ep 1 (Old vs New).** Build only this one. Use it to lock the character: rig, bounce timing, shared open/close, sound kit, type style, 16:9 and 9:16 framing.
2. **Review and perfect the pilot** until the square feels alive and the open/close frames match exactly.
3. **Expand:** every later episode reuses the locked rig, open, close and sound kit, so only its middle section is new work.
4. **Optional trailer** cut from the finished episodes.

Nothing past the pilot gets animated until the pilot is approved.

## Character rules (all episodes)

- **Shape:** always a square at rest. Squash, stretch and skew only, never rounded into a circle.
- **Bounce:** landings squash ~20% for 3 frames, overshoot, settle in 6–8 frames.
- **Colour:** logo red when healthy; washed-out and soft-edged only in the old-workflow episode.
- **Shared open (≈1.5s):** logo still → square wiggles → squash-hop out of its slot.
- **Shared close (≈2s):** square arcs back, overshoots, small mid-air correction, drops in, squash, settle. Last frame = first frame. A short CTA line ("Download free · Mac · Windows") fades in under the logo and out again before the final frame, so the frames match exactly.
- **Sound:** the same opening tap and closing tap in every episode.

Format: 16:9 (1920×1080) and 9:16 (1080×1920), 30 fps.
