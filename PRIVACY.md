# Chromasmith Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 5 October 2026**

This policy describes the Windows desktop version of Chromasmith, including the version distributed through the Microsoft Store. It also applies to the browser version where the same features are used. “Chromasmith,” “we,” and “us” refer to the publisher of the app.

## Summary

Chromasmith is designed to process photos and other media on your device. We do not operate a Chromasmith account service or receive your photos, edits, or library catalog. The Windows app stores app data and caches locally. Data is sent to Google or Adobe only when you choose to use the relevant optional integration.

## Information the app accesses and processes

### Photos, video, and files

When you open media or choose a folder for the Library, Chromasmith reads the files needed for editing and library features. Processing, including RAW decoding and image analysis, runs on your device. Exports are written to the destination you select. Chromasmith does not upload local media to a Chromasmith-operated server.

The Windows app may read image metadata, such as dimensions, capture date, camera and lens information, ratings, and location metadata when present, to display or support editing and organization features. When you choose to save edits or metadata, the app writes the relevant output to the location you select. Library scanning is initiated by your folder selection; cataloging records file and folder paths and supported metadata about discovered media.

### Data stored on your Windows device

The app stores settings, editing recipes, presets, session state, library configuration and catalog data in app-specific local storage. It also creates local thumbnails and image-processing caches to make browsing and editing faster. These may contain derived image previews as well as file paths and metadata. They are not sent to us.

The app stores its application data under Windows app data locations for Chromasmith (including app data and local cache locations). Some data, such as catalogs or user-created library information, may remain until you remove it. Cache files can be regenerated, but deleting app data may remove local settings, catalogs, albums, or other app state. You can remove the app and its data using Windows controls; Windows may retain app data unless you choose the option to delete it. You can also remove files from the app's data folders directly after closing the app.

### Credentials and tokens

If you configure Google or Adobe integrations, the client identifiers and any client secrets you enter are kept in local app storage. Short-lived authorization tokens are held in local app/session storage for the integration to work. Chromasmith does not receive these values. Anyone with access to your Windows account and app data may be able to access locally stored integration information. Disconnect the integration in Chromasmith and revoke its access with the provider to stop future access.

## Optional third-party services

These integrations are optional and require you to configure credentials and sign in. When used, the app communicates directly with the provider; that provider's privacy terms apply to its handling of the information.

### Google Photos

If you connect Google Photos, Chromasmith uses Google OAuth and the Google Photos Picker. Google receives authentication and API requests. The picker lets you select media; the app retrieves the selected items for local editing. If you enable “Upload exports,” the app sends the exports you create to your Google Photos account, in a Chromasmith album. Google handles this information under its [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can disconnect in Chromasmith and revoke Chromasmith's access in your [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

### Adobe Lightroom

The Windows app can connect to Adobe Lightroom cloud services if you configure an Adobe API client and sign in. Adobe receives authentication and API requests; Chromasmith may retrieve account/catalog information and the Lightroom photos you select for import. Any photo you import is then processed locally. Adobe handles its information under its [Privacy Policy](https://www.adobe.com/privacy/policy.html). You can disconnect in Chromasmith and revoke the app's access through your Adobe account settings.

### Microsoft Store and Windows

Microsoft distributes and installs the Store version and may process information about Store downloads, installations, licensing, updates, and app usage under Microsoft's own privacy terms. Windows may also process app diagnostics or crash information according to your Windows diagnostic settings. Chromasmith does not receive Store account or Windows diagnostic data through an app-operated analytics service.

## Network activity

The Windows app does not require a Chromasmith account and has no Chromasmith-operated analytics, advertising, or telemetry service. Apart from network activity needed to retrieve app resources if applicable, the app contacts Google or Adobe when you use their integrations. Microsoft Store and Windows may perform their own distribution, licensing, update, security, or diagnostic communications outside the app's control.

## How information is used, disclosed, and secured

Local information is used to provide editing, library, export, and preference features. It is stored on your device under your Windows account and is subject to the security of that device and account. We do not have a Chromasmith server that stores this information, and we do not sell or share it. Information involved in a Google or Adobe integration is disclosed directly to that provider as needed to carry out the feature you requested. Microsoft and Windows may separately process information as described above.

## Your choices and privacy requests

You choose which files and folders to open or scan, where to save exports, and whether to connect Google or Adobe. You can disconnect those integrations and revoke provider access. You can remove local app data using the Windows controls described above. Since Chromasmith does not receive or hold your local files or app catalog, we cannot retrieve, correct, or delete that local data for you.
